# Market Research: JRPG / RPG Candidates

This document lists 3-4 JRPG/RPG titles relevant to Vale Village v2's design profile, with authoritative links and concise rationale mapping mechanics and UX to the project.

1) Golden Sun (Camelot / Nintendo)
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Sun
- Rationale: Direct inspiration for this project; strong emphasis on overworld exploration, puzzle-based dungeons, and an elemental "Djinn"-like system that affects party stats and combat roles. Useful for mapping party composition, puzzle integration into world traversal, and merging exploration with combat-affecting resources.

2) Chrono Trigger (Square / Enix)
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chrono_Trigger
- Rationale: Exemplary pacing and encounter design, with streamlined menu flows, visible enemy encounters in the overworld, and dynamic combo mechanics. Relevant for designing low-friction battle entry/exit, scripted combo reactions, and memorable boss encounters that emphasize player choice.

3) Final Fantasy VI (Square / Enix)
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VI
- Rationale: Strong example of layered party systems (unique character abilities, magic/item economies) and dramatic mechanical moments (e.g., set-piece battles). Use as guidance for character progression systems, distinct role definitions, and integrating narrative beats with gameplay mechanics.

4) Octopath Traveler (Square Enix)
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/921260/Octopath_Traveler/
- Rationale: Modern JRPG that balances HD-2D presentation with clear, tactile combat UX and distinct class/ability synergies. Useful for thinking about presentation, clarity of combat feedback, and designing distinct character archetypes that feel impactful.

Notes / Next actions:
- These candidates cover both direct inspiration (Golden Sun) and complementary design exemplars (Chrono Trigger, FFVI, Octopath) for mechanics, UX, and presentation.
- If preferred, swap Octopath for Dragon Quest XI or Persona series to prioritize different UX tradeoffs (open exploration vs character-driven social systems).
